## What to Include When Asking for Help

To get the fastest response, include:

- **OS version:** Windows 10/11, build number
- **Antigravity version:** Check in Help > About
- **Proxy tool:** Clash, V2Ray, corporate proxy, etc.
- **Proxy settings:** Your `proxy.settings.txt` content (hide sensitive IPs if needed)
- **Error messages:** Exact text of any errors
- **Proxy log:** Content of `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Antigravity\logs\proxy-YYYYMMDD.log`
- **Steps to reproduce:** What you did, what happened, what you expected
